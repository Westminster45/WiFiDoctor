# Code of conduct

Everyone taking part in this project, in issues, pull requests or discussions, is expected to be respectful and constructive.

## Expected behaviour

- Be courteous to other people, whatever their background or level of experience.
- Keep discussion focused on the project and on the work, not on the person.
- Accept that decisions about the project are made by the maintainer.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory language or content.
- Publishing other people's private information without their permission.
- Spam, or repeatedly posting off-topic content.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close issues or pull requests, and block people who do not follow this code of conduct.

To report a concern privately, contact the maintainer through their GitHub profile.
